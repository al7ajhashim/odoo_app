Product Cost History

Know when, why and by whom the cost of each product variant changed

Why this module?

With the average cost (AVCO) method, the product cost changes on every receipt, customer return, negative stock compensation or manual update, but Odoo 18 keeps no history of these changes: the cost of a product on a past date, and the operation that changed it, can no longer be found.

Product Cost History records every change of the cost, at the moment it happens, for each product variant and each company, with its source and its reference.

The base for a "cost at a date" report. Because every change is recorded with its date, the cost of any product on any past day is simply the last recorded cost before the end of that day. This history is the reliable source for any report that needs the product cost on a given date: stock valuation at a date, cost of past sales, margin analysis or stock ageing with cost.

Main features

  • Every change is logged: any write of the cost is caught, whatever changed it.
  • Known source: receipt, customer return, quantity correction, negative stock compensation, delivery with lot valuation, manual update, import, product creation.
  • Reference of the document that changed the cost (receipt, return, compensated deliveries).
  • One line per receipt: a receipt with several lines of the same product gives one line, from the cost before the receipt to the cost after it.
  • Only real changes: nothing is logged when the cost does not change.
  • Per variant and company: each variant keeps its own history in each company.
  • Opening cost recorded on install for every variant in every company.
  • Technical origin (module and method) for the administrator, so that any future customization that changes the cost is identified.
  • Read-only for everybody: lines cannot be created, edited or deleted.
  • Multi-company safe, Arabic translation included.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18.0, Community or Enterprise, with the Inventory app (the module depends on stock_account).

Costing methods

  • Average Cost (AVCO): fully supported and tested, every source of the table below is recognised.
  • Standard Price: the cost only changes when it is updated by a user or an import, and these changes are logged as Manual Update or Import.
  • First In First Out (FIFO): every change is logged. With FIFO, Odoo also recomputes the cost after each delivery: these lines currently show the source Other (a dedicated source is planned for a next version).

Installation and configuration

1. Install the module

  1. Copy the folder bk_product_cost_history to your addons path and restart the Odoo server (Odoo.sh: add it to your repository).
  2. Activate the developer mode (see step 2), go to Apps, click Update Apps List, search Product Cost History and click Activate.
  3. On install, the opening cost of every variant is recorded at once, in every company (and the history from the sales invoices, if available: see below).

2. Give the access to the users

The history is shown to three kinds of users:

  • the Administrators (Settings access),
  • the users with Show Full Accounting Features (accountants),
  • the users of the group Inventory: View Product Cost History.

To add a user to this group:

  1. Settings: at the bottom of the page, click Activate the developer mode.
  2. Settings › Users & Companies › Users: open the user.
  3. Tab Access Rights, section TECHNICAL: tick Inventory: View Product Cost History and save.

Nothing else to configure: the history is recorded from the install date.

How to use it

The Cost History tab of a product

Open a product (Inventory › Products › Products, or from Sales, Purchase...), tab Cost History: every change of its cost, the newest first. The current cost is the Cost field of the product. On a product with several variants, the variant of each line is shown.

The history of one variant

Open the variant (Variants button of the product, or Inventory › Products › Product Variants when the variants are enabled): the same tab shows the lines of this variant only.

All the details when needed

The Reference, the Quantity and the Technical Origin (administrators only) are optional columns, hidden by default: click the settings icon at the right of the column titles to show them.

Click a line to see all its details in a read-only window.

How to read the history

The history of the variant [DPX1-BLK] above, read from the oldest line (bottom) to the newest (top):

SourceOld → NewQtyWhat happened
Receipt0.00 → 900.0010 First receipt: 10 units at 900.
Receipt900.00 → 950.0010 10 units at 1,000: (10 × 900 + 10 × 1,000) / 20 = 950.
Receipt950.00 → 987.505 5 units were delivered (a delivery does not change the average cost, so it has no line), then 5 units received at 1,100: (15 × 950 + 5 × 1,100) / 20 = 987.50.
Manual Update987.50 → 980.000 A user changed the Cost field of the product.
Customer Return980.00 → 978.571 1 unit came back at its delivery cost (950): (20 × 980 + 1 × 950) / 21 = 978.57.
  • Old Cost of a line is always the New Cost of the previous line of the same variant and company.
  • Quantity is the quantity of the operation (in the unit of measure of the product); it is 0 when the change is not linked to a quantity (manual update, import, ...).
  • Deliveries ← Receipt (negative stock compensation): the deliveries made before the product was received, then the receipt that revalued them.

Sources of a cost change

SourceWhenReference
ReceiptA receipt (or an inventory adjustment) updates the average costReceipt
Customer ReturnA returned product comes back at its sale costReturn
Quantity CorrectionThe done quantity of a validated receipt is changedReceipt
Negative Stock CompensationA receipt revalues products delivered before they were receivedDeliveries ← Receipt
Delivery (Lot Valuation)With the valuation by lot/serial number, a delivery recomputes the product costDelivery
Manual UpdateThe Cost field of the product is changed—
ImportThe cost is imported—
Product CreationA product is created with a cost—
Opening CostThe cost on the install date—
Historical from Sales InvoicesPast costs taken from the sales invoices on install (see below)Invoice
OtherAny other change, e.g. landed costs, a vendor bill price difference, a revaluation or a FIFO delivery (see the technical origin)—

Historical from Sales Invoices: when the sales invoice lines carry the product cost in a product_cost field (a common customization), the install also builds the past history from the posted sales invoices. Without that field, only the opening cost is recorded.

Good to know

  • The history starts on the install date: the past changes cannot be rebuilt (except from the sales invoices, see above); the opening cost line gives the cost of each variant on that date.
  • Landed costs, vendor bill price differences and revaluations are logged with the source Other; the Technical Origin column shows the module that changed the cost.
  • Multi-company: the cost of a product is kept per company; each line belongs to the company in which the cost changed, and users only see the lines of their allowed companies.
  • Read-only: nobody, administrators included, can create, edit or delete a line.
  • Languages: English and Arabic are included.
Uninstalling the module deletes the whole cost history. Export it first if you need to keep it.

Frequently asked questions

I do not see the Cost History tab.
Your user needs one of the accesses of configuration step 2.

A delivery has no line, is it normal?
Yes with the average cost: a delivery does not change the average cost. With the valuation by lot/serial number or with FIFO, Odoo recomputes the cost after a delivery, and this change is logged.

Why does a receipt with several lines give a single line?
The history shows the cost before and after the whole receipt, with the total received quantity.

Does it slow down the operations?
No: a line is only written when the cost really changes.

Need the cost of each lot or serial number?

With the Odoo 18 valuation by lot / serial number, each lot keeps its own cost. Lot Cost History records the history of the cost of every lot and serial number, with the same sources, references and quantities.
