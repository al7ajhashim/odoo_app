Lot Cost History

Know when, why and by whom the cost of each lot or serial number changed

Why this module?

Odoo 18 can value the stock by lot / serial number: each lot keeps its own cost, updated by its receipts, returns, corrections, revaluations and manual updates. But Odoo keeps no history of these changes: the cost of a serial number on a past date, and the operation that changed it, can no longer be found.

Lot Cost History records every change of the cost of a lot, at the moment it happens, for each company, with its source, its reference and its quantity.

The real margin of each serial number. Because every change is recorded with its date, the cost of any lot or serial number on any past day is simply the last recorded cost before the end of that day: the exact base for the margin of a sold serial number, a valuation by lot at a date, or an audit of the cost of an expensive item.

Main features

  • Every change is logged: any write of the cost of a lot is caught, whatever changed it.
  • Known source: receipt, customer return, quantity correction, negative stock compensation, manual update, import, product cost update, revaluation, valuation settings change.
  • Reference and quantity of the document that changed the cost.
  • Clean start: the temporary cost Odoo gives a new lot is not logged, the first line goes from zero to the real cost of its receipt.
  • Only real changes: nothing is logged when the cost does not change.
  • Per lot and company: each lot keeps its own history in each company.
  • Opening cost recorded on install for every lot that has a cost, even without stock.
  • Fast: about two database queries per serial number on a receipt (a receipt of 1,000 serial numbers takes less than one second more).
  • Read-only for everybody, multi-company safe, Arabic translation included.
  • Tested: automated tests for every source, access right and unit of measure.

Requirements

  • Odoo 18.0, Community or Enterprise, with the Inventory app.
  • The free module Product Cost History (bk_product_cost_history): it is installed automatically with this module.
  • Products tracked by lots or serial numbers and valued by lot/serial number (a standard Odoo 18 option, see the configuration below). Products that are not valued by lot are not logged by this module (their product cost is logged by Product Cost History).

Costing methods

  • Average Cost (AVCO): fully supported and tested, every source of the table below is recognised.
  • First In First Out (FIFO): every change of a lot cost is logged; the sources are those of the table below.
  • Standard Price: the cost of a lot only changes when it is updated by a user, an import or a revaluation, and these changes are logged.

Installation and configuration

1. Install the module

  1. Copy the folders bk_lot_cost_history and bk_product_cost_history to your addons path and restart the Odoo server (Odoo.sh: add them to your repository).
  2. Activate the developer mode (see step 4), go to Apps, click Update Apps List, search Lot Cost History and click Activate.
  3. On install, the opening cost of every lot that has a cost is recorded at once, in every company.

2. Enable the lots and serial numbers (if not done yet)

  1. Inventory › Configuration › Settings, section Traceability.
  2. Tick Lots & Serial Numbers and click Save.

3. Value the products by lot / serial number

  1. Inventory › Configuration › Product Categories: open the category of the product and set the Costing Method to Average Cost (AVCO) or First In First Out (FIFO).
  2. Inventory › Products › Products: open the product, tab General Information, tick Track Inventory and choose By Lots or By Unique Serial Number.
  3. Tick Valuation by Lot/Serial number (shown under Track Inventory) and save.
Odoo refuses to change the costing method of a product category whose products are valued by lot and have stock: choose the costing method before receiving the products.

4. Give the access to the users

The history is shown to three kinds of users:

  • the Administrators (Settings access),
  • the users with Show Full Accounting Features (accountants),
  • the users of the group Inventory: View Product Cost History.

To add a user to this group:

  1. Settings: at the bottom of the page, click Activate the developer mode.
  2. Settings › Users & Companies › Users: open the user.
  3. Tab Access Rights, section TECHNICAL: tick Inventory: View Product Cost History and save.

The same group also gives access to the product cost history of the free module. The Inventory › Reporting menu is shown by Odoo to the Inventory Administrators only: the other users of the group open the history from the product button and the lot tab (see below).

How to use it

The history of one lot or serial number

Inventory › Products › Lots/Serial Numbers, open a lot, tab Cost History. The current cost of the lot is the Cost field of the form; the tab shows how it got there, the newest change first. The tab is shown once the lot has at least one line.

The Reference, the Quantity and the Technical Origin (administrators only) are optional columns: click the settings icon at the right of the column titles to show them.

Click a line to see all its details in a read-only window.

All the lots of a product

On a product valued by lot, the Lot Costs button shows the number of history lines of its lots and opens them. When the form has many buttons, Odoo puts it under More.

The full report

Inventory › Reporting › Lot Cost History lists all the changes of all the lots.

Open the search options to:

  • search a lot, a product, a reference or a user,
  • filter on a period (Date), the Receipts (receipts and customer returns) or the Manual Updates (manual, import and product cost updates),
  • group by product, lot, source, company or date.

Grouped by source, for example, the report shows how many changes each source made; open a group to see its lines.

To export the lines to Excel, select them (tick the box at the top left of the list), then Actions › Export.

How to read the history

The history of the lot LOT-A above, read from the oldest line (bottom) to the newest (top):

SourceOld → NewQtyWhat happened
Receipt0.00 → 50.0010 First receipt of the lot: 10 units at 50. The first line always starts from zero.
Receipt50.00 → 60.005 5 units were delivered, then 5 more were received at 70: (5 × 50 + 5 × 70) / 10 = 60. The delivery itself does not change the cost of the lot, so it has no line.
Customer Return60.00 → 59.091 1 unit came back at its delivery cost (50): (10 × 60 + 1 × 50) / 11 = 59.09.
Receipt59.09 → 78.5710 10 units received at 100: (11 × 59.09 + 10 × 100) / 21 = 78.57.
Quantity Correction78.57 → 82.695 The done quantity of the same receipt was changed from 10 to 15: 5 more units at 100.
  • Old Cost of a line is always the New Cost of the previous line of the same lot and company.
  • Quantity is the quantity of the lot in the operation (in the unit of measure of the product); it is 0 when the change is not linked to a quantity (manual update, revaluation, ...).
  • Deliveries ← Receipt (negative stock compensation): the deliveries made before the lot was received, then the receipt that revalued them.

Sources of a cost change

SourceWhenReference
ReceiptA receipt (or an inventory adjustment) of the lot updates its average costReceipt
Customer ReturnA returned lot comes back at its delivery costReturn
Quantity CorrectionThe done quantity of a validated receipt is changedReceipt
Negative Stock CompensationA receipt revalues a lot delivered before it was receivedDeliveries ← Receipt
Manual UpdateThe Cost field of the lot is changed—
ImportThe cost of the lot is imported—
Product Cost UpdateThe cost of the product is changed: Odoo writes it on all its lots—
RevaluationThe stock valuation of the lot or of the product is adjusted with the Odoo revaluation wizardReason of the revaluation
Valuation Settings ChangeThe valuation by lot is enabled on a product: its lots get the product cost—
Opening CostThe cost on the install date—
OtherAny other change, e.g. landed costs or a vendor bill price difference (see the technical origin)—

Good to know

  • The history starts on the install date: the past changes of the lots cannot be rebuilt, the opening cost line gives the cost of each lot on that date.
  • Landed costs and vendor bill price differences change the cost of the lots through other Odoo modules (stock_landed_costs, purchase_stock): they are logged with the source Other, and the Technical Origin column shows the module that changed the cost.
  • Multi-company: the cost of a lot is kept per company; each line belongs to the company in which the cost changed, and users only see the lines of their allowed companies.
  • Read-only: nobody, administrators included, can create, edit or delete a line.
  • Languages: English and Arabic are included.
Uninstalling the module deletes the whole lot cost history. Export it first if you need to keep it.

Frequently asked questions

I do not see the Cost History tab on a lot.
Check that the product is valued by lot (configuration step 3), that your user has the access (step 4), and that the lot has at least one history line (the tab is hidden while it is empty).

I do not see the Lot Costs button on the product.
It is only shown on products valued by lot, and it may be under the More button.

I do not see the Lot Cost History menu.
Odoo shows the Inventory Reporting menu to the Inventory Administrators only. The other users open the history from the product button or the lot tab.

Why does the first line of a lot start from 0?
When a lot is created, Odoo gives it the cost of the product until its receipt sets its real cost. This temporary cost is not a real cost of the lot, so the history starts from zero with the real cost.

A delivery has no line, is it normal?
Yes: delivering a lot does not change its cost, only the received operations, the corrections and the updates do.

Does it slow down the receipts?
No: about two database queries per serial number, less than one second more for a receipt of 1,000 serial numbers.
